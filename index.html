My story

How this work found me

I grew up an only child, raised by a single mother who lived with depression, anxiety and bouts of agoraphobia. I learned two things young: what it feels like to carry something alone, and that some ways of healing don't show up in textbooks.

I went the textbook route anyway. I studied psychology, earned my MSW, and spent more than a decade inside New York City's child welfare and residential systems. I sat with parents fighting to get their children back from foster care. I built a group home for teenage boys most programs wouldn't take. I learned how systems work, and where they fail the people inside them.

In 2014, a psychic reading led me to kundalini yoga, crystals and other metaphysical tools for my own healing. Years later, an email introduced me to Trauma Resolution Energy Therapy (TRET), and the two halves of my life finally met.

I've watched people understand their trauma completely and still feel stuck. Sometimes the hurt lives where talking alone doesn't reach.

TRET works with the body's energy system, its meridians and chakras, to resolve the root of a trauma response. Most people have never heard that this kind of therapy exists. I want more people, clinicians included, to know there's another way through.
